## Identity review notes

- Asprinio is already covered by the existing Greco article and its aliases.
- Gual is grouped with Malvasia Fina, following Cabildo de Tenerife's varietal
  account. Check local homonyms before adding aliases.
- Mammolo and Sciaccarello retain separate assignments after source review.
  The 2021 Parentage Atlas found the registered Mammolo clone I-MAM-PA-1
  genetically distinct from Termarina/Sciaccarello, while noting other Tuscan
  material called Mammolo. Both assignments must explain this homonym problem.
- Schiava Grossa/Trollinger has one assignment. The GrapeGen catalogue also
  applies Vernatsch to Schiava Gentile and Schiava Grigia; an unqualified
  Vernatsch alias would conceal that ambiguity.
- Negramoll's assignment includes the supplied Tinta Negra/Tinta Negra Mole and
  Mollar names, with genetic verification required and Algarve Negra Mole flagged
  for distinction. Miles Madeira supplies a regional account, not sufficient
  genetic evidence by itself.
- Alfrocheiro's brief includes Baboso Negro and Bruñal. The supplied 2015
  minor-cultivar paper does not discuss Alfrocheiro in its searchable text; use
  the dedicated Cunha et al. study below instead.
- Other briefs flag Cornalin/Humagne Rouge, Pignoletto/Grechetto Gentile,
  Douce Noire/Bonarda/Charbono, Forastera names, and the three Torrontés targets
  for careful scope and synonym review. All assignments require identity checks.

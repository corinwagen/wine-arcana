# Rondinella

Rondinella is a red grape of the Verona area, particularly associated with
[Valpolicella](../regions/valpolicella.md) and Bardolino. It is usually
encountered in blends with [Corvina](corvina.md) and
[Corvinone](corvinone.md), rather than under its own name on the label.
Its importance includes dependable vineyard production and suitability for
drying, as well as what it contributes to a fresh-grape wine.

## A useful blending variety

The Valpolicella specification consolidated in 2023 places Rondinella at
5–30% of the vineyard composition.[^rules] That is a substantial, continuing
role. A minor position on a label does not mean the grape is merely a
historical leftover.

Speri's published Valpolicella Classico blend illustrates the point: it
combines Corvina with 30% Rondinella and 10% [Molinara](molinara.md).
The wine matures briefly in steel and glass-lined concrete, providing a
reference for the fresh-grape side of the region rather than prolonged
grape drying or new-oak flavour. Its character belongs to the whole blend;
the proportions do not allow each aroma to be assigned to one ingredient.

## Vineyard behaviour and drying

Vivai Cooperativi Rauscedo describes Rondinella as vigorous and adaptable,
with dependable yields, medium-to-late ripening, and thick-skinned berries.
It also identifies drought susceptibility. Adaptability therefore needs
qualification: a vine that performs reliably across several soils still
has limits under water stress.

The nursery highlights its ability to withstand extended drying in
*fruttai*, the drying rooms used for
[appassimento](../concepts/appassimento.md). That makes it relevant to
[Amarone](../styles/amarone-della-valpolicella.md) and Recioto as well as
lighter reds. Fruit condition, room humidity, and airflow remain part of
the result. Suitability for drying is a useful trait, not immunity to rot.

## Wine character

Rondinella alone is described by the nursery as moderately acidic, not
especially tannic, and capable of contributing fruit and colour. Its
different clone profiles nevertheless vary in structure and pigmentation.
The appropriate expectation is a contribution to a blend, rather than
one fixed intensity of colour or [tannin](../concepts/tannin.md).

In standardized University of Verona vinifications, Rondinella belonged to
the comparatively less phenolic local group, alongside Corvina, Corvinone,
and Molinara. This helps qualify the shorthand that its only purpose is
to “add colour”: [Oseleta](oseleta.md), for example, occupied a quite
different phenolic position in that experiment. Vineyard selection and
winemaking remain relevant when moving from experimental wines to bottles.

[^rules]: MASAF, Valpolicella DOC production specification, consolidated
    3 August 2023, article 2.

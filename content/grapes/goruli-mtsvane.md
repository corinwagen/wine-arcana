# Goruli Mtsvane

Goruli Mtsvane is a white Georgian wine grape associated with Kartli,
including the vineyards around Gori and the Ateni Valley. It is distinct
from [Mtsvane Kakhuri](mtsvane-kakhuri.md), despite the shared word
*mtsvane*, meaning green. The regional qualifier is essential when
identifying the grape.

It appears in still white wines and in sparkling blends with
[Chinuri](chinuri.md).

## Vineyard identity

The Georgian Scientific-Research Center of Agriculture's varietal record
describes yellow-green berries, medium-density bunches, and strong shoot
growth. These physical characteristics help identify the vine but do not
prescribe the eventual wine's flavour.

## Freshness, blending, and maturation

The National Wine Agency notes sensitivity to
[oxidation](../concepts/oxidation.md) and a role in Atenuri sparkling blends.
Oxygen management matters when preserving fresh aromas is the intention.

Varietal production gives another view. MWINERY documents a dry wine made
entirely from Goruli Mtsvane grown in Shida Kartli. Its account describes
citrus fruit, fresh [acidity](../concepts/acidity.md), and a modest oak
contribution. The example shows a contemporary producer putting the grape
on the label while allowing maturation to influence its expression.

[Oak maturation](../concepts/oak-maturation.md) can add aromas and change
texture, so a wooded wine should not be used to assign every sensory
detail to the grape. Comparing it with a young, unwooded white or a
Chinuri blend helps separate those choices.

# Raboso Veronese

Raboso Veronese is a red grape associated particularly with the eastern
Veneto around Treviso and Venice. Despite its name, it should not be placed
automatically among the principal grapes of Verona's
[Valpolicella](../regions/valpolicella.md). Its most useful comparison is
with [Raboso Piave](raboso-piave.md): the two are related but distinct, and
both belong to the Piave area's wine traditions.

## Parent and offspring

Genetic research by Manna Crespan and colleagues identified Raboso Veronese
as the offspring of Raboso Piave and the white grape Marzemina Bianca.
Nuclear and chloroplast markers identified Raboso Piave as the maternal
parent.[^parentage] The shared name therefore reflects a real family
connection, but not identity. Raboso Veronese is not simply a clone of Piave.

The Italian Vitis Database maintains a separate entry and national
registration for the variety. This distinction matters when a wine label
uses the shorter word *Raboso*: the producer's technical information may
be needed to establish which vine, or combination of vines, supplied it.

## Vineyard and wine differences

Moletto, a producer at Motta di Livenza, describes thick-skinned berries in
compact bunches and a late harvest. In its comparison, Veronese matures
earlier than Piave and gives less acidic, less tannic fruit while retaining
substantial colour. The comparison is useful, but “less than Piave” does
not imply low acidity or an inherently soft wine.

For a grower, relative earliness can matter when a late season turns wet.
It does not remove the influence of site, crop size, and weather, or make
the two grapes interchangeable. For the cellar, differences in acid and
phenolic composition affect how much
[maceration](../concepts/maceration.md) and maturation suit a particular lot.

## Contemporary place in the Piave area

Moletto reports preserving and propagating vines already present when the
estate acquired its land in 1960. That continuity gives a concrete example
of the variety's eastern Veneto identity and a producer reference for
exploring it today.

The Malanotte del Piave specification published by Consorzio Vini Venezia
also provides a formal place for Raboso Veronese: up to 30% alongside a
minimum 70% Raboso Piave, subject to the specification's limited substitution
allowance. The wine combines fresh and dried-grape components and requires
extended maturation. These rules establish a role for Veronese in a
particular blend; they do not require every Raboso wine to undergo
[appassimento](../concepts/appassimento.md).

# Léon Millot

Léon Millot is a red [hybrid grape](../concepts/hybrid-grapes.md) whose early
ripening makes it useful in cool regions. Its French breeding history
connects it to [Maréchal Foch](marechal-foch.md), but vineyards in New York
and Nova Scotia give it a continuing identity of its own. Dark colour need
not mean a stern wine: Keuka Lake Vineyards, for example, describes a
combination of colour, gentle tannin and freshness.

## Origins

The French Plantgrape catalogue records a cross between a seedling of
101-14 MGt and Goldriesling. The first parent derives from rootstock
material; this is interspecific ancestry, rather than simply a crossing
of two familiar European wine grapes.[^identity]

Millot and Foch offer a useful comparison within the same breeding family.
Perennia's Nova Scotia guide describes Millot as more vigorous and
productive, approximately a week earlier to ripen locally, but somewhat
less winter-hardy. A shorter ripening season and greater
[winter hardiness](../concepts/winter-hardiness.md) are different advantages.
Which matters more depends on the vineyard.

## Growth and disease

Plantgrape describes strong growth, a spreading habit and suitability for
long pruning. It records good sugar accumulation, resistance to
[downy mildew](../concepts/downy-mildew.md) and susceptibility to
[powdery mildew](../concepts/powdery-mildew.md). Resistance to one organism
therefore does not justify treating the vine as generally disease-proof.
The distinction is particularly important when interpreting the broad
phrase [disease resistance](../concepts/disease-resistance.md).

Keuka Lake Vineyards offers a practical illustration of managing growth:
its account describes Guyot training with long canes to distribute vigour
and balance fruit against vegetation. That is a documented estate practice,
not a universal prescription for every Millot vineyard.

## Wines in New York and Nova Scotia

In the [Finger Lakes](../regions/finger-lakes.md), Keuka Lake Vineyards'
2024 bottling combines fruit from two blocks and two selections identified
by the producer as Boordy and Foster. The estate traces one block to
Charles Fournier's plantings in the 1950s. Its account makes Millot part
of the region's living vineyard history, alongside the vinifera grapes
that receive more attention today.[^keuka]

The same grape also works within blends. Jost's Nova Scotia 4 Skins wine
combines Millot with Foch, Castel and Lucie Kuhlmann. Reading these two
examples together gives a fuller picture than a fixed list of aromas:
Millot can be the subject of a varietal wine or one contributor to a
regional red blend. [Tannin](../concepts/tannin.md), ripeness and cellar
handling still shape the finished wine.

[^identity]: Plantgrape, “Léon millot,” origin and parentage.
[^keuka]: Keuka Lake Vineyards, “2024 Leon Millot,” vineyard and training

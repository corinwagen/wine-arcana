# Mandilaria

Mandilaria is a dark-skinned Greek wine grape associated with Crete and the
Aegean islands. Its deep colour, firm [tannin](../concepts/tannin.md), and
pronounced [acidity](../concepts/acidity.md) can accompany relatively modest
alcohol and body. That combination explains its usefulness in blends: it
can give grip and freshness to a partner that supplies more weight.

Mandilari is a familiar name on Crete. There, its established partner is
[Kotsifali](kotsifali.md), whose softer texture and fuller fruit complement
Mandilaria's structure. The pairing is associated with Peza and Archanes.

## Ripening and structure

Mandilaria ripens late, and crop management matters. Alexakis describes a
vigorous, productive vine whose tannin maturity can suffer when yields are
too high. The producer also identifies susceptibility to downy mildew and
grey rot. Vineyard conditions therefore affect whether waiting for riper
tannins is practical, as well as how much fruit the vine can carry.

Dark colour is a poor guide to either alcoholic strength or drinking
readiness. With Mandilaria, a deeply coloured wine can still feel lean and
firm. Blending can change that balance, while careful
[maceration](../concepts/maceration.md)—the contact between juice and grape
solids—helps determine how much additional tannin reaches the wine.

## Contemporary Cretan expressions

Varietal bottlings show how growers and winemakers handle the grape without
a softer blending partner. Lyrarakis's Plakoura 2021 came from an upland
vineyard at about 500 metres. The producer reports seven days of
fermentation and maceration below 27°C specifically to limit extraction,
followed by maturation in new and used oak.[^1] The short extraction is a
useful part of the example: concentration in the fruit does not require
prolonged contact with the skins.

Lyrarakis also documents a varietal Mandilari
[rosé](../styles/rose.md) from central Crete. These two wines demonstrate
different uses for the same grape, from a fresh pink wine to a structured
red with [oak maturation](../concepts/oak-maturation.md). Alongside the
traditional Kotsifali blend, they make cellar choices central to understanding
present-day Mandilaria.

Beyond Crete, Alexakis records the grape on
[Santorini](../regions/santorini.md), Paros, and Rhodes.

[^1]: Lyrarakis, “Mandilari Plakoura 2021,” vineyard and winemaking record.

# Saperavi

Saperavi is a Georgian red wine grape closely associated with Kakheti in
the country's east. It is a teinturier: pigment occurs in its flesh as
well as its skin, as it does in [Alicante Bouschet](alicante-bouschet.md).
That makes deep colour one of its most readily recognisable properties.
Its wines can also combine marked [acidity](../concepts/acidity.md) with
substantial [tannin](../concepts/tannin.md).

Georgia remains its central reference point, but New York's
[Finger Lakes](../regions/finger-lakes.md) provides an important contemporary
comparison. There, growers have used Saperavi to make deeply coloured reds
in a region more widely associated with white wine and lighter red varieties.

## Colour and extraction

Coloured pulp gives the juice a source of pigment before a winemaker
decides how vigorously to extract the skins. Tannin extraction remains a
separate consideration. The length and intensity of
[maceration](../concepts/maceration.md) can therefore change the wine's grip
without being the sole explanation for its appearance.

Saperavi can be made in qvevri, the large clay vessels used in Georgian
winemaking, or in tanks followed by other maturation vessels. The National
Wine Agency describes much shorter skin contact for red qvevri wines than
for the extended contact often used with Georgian whites. A red Saperavi
and a [skin-contact white wine](../styles/skin-contact-white-wine.md) can
share a vessel tradition while following different extraction schedules.

## The Finger Lakes connection

Saperavi's New York history is associated with Konstantin Frank, whose
work helped establish vinifera grapes in the Finger Lakes. Recent reporting
describes renewed grower interest and exchange with Georgian winemakers
through Saperica, founded in 2022. It also records frost and freeze losses:
cold tolerance is useful, but it does not remove the region's vintage risks.[^1]

Dr. Konstantin Frank's 2023 Saperavi illustrates the local use of warmer
sites. The winery reports fruit chiefly from Pendleton Farms near the
southern end of Canandaigua Lake, with the remainder from its Seneca Lake
estate. It fermented the wine in open-top steel tanks and matured it in
French oak. The example connects site selection with a structured red style,
while distinguishing [oak maturation](../concepts/oak-maturation.md) from
the grape's colour and acidity.

[^1]: Hannah Walhout, “Is Saperavi the Next Great Finger Lakes Grape?”,
    reporting on Konstantin Frank, Saperica, and Barbichette.

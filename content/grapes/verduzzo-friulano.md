# Verduzzo Friulano

Verduzzo Friulano is a white grape of northeastern Italy with an unusual
combination of golden fruit, body and perceptible tannic texture. Its wines
include dry versions, but the sweet wines of Ramandolo are central to its
identity. Sugar and a little astringency can coexist in the same mouthful.

## Skins, hills and harvest

Rauscedo describes thick-skinned fruit and ripening in the middle-to-later
part of the season. The vine benefits from well-exposed hills and relatively
dry conditions, while very fertile sites can work against the intended
balance. Its thick skins give some tolerance of bunch rot, without making
the crop immune to disease.

The nursery distinguishes lighter dry wines from the plains from the more
substantial sweet wines associated with hillsides. That is a useful regional
tendency, not a rule that determines every bottle. Picking maturity,
concentration and the handling of the skins all affect the result.

## Understanding Ramandolo

Around Nimis and Tarcento, Ramandolo gives Verduzzo a concentrated local
expression. The Oro di Ramandolo producers' association describes a golden
passito whose sweetness is balanced by acidity and the grape's tannic texture.
Its account follows late-October harvesting with drying in boxes or on racks
before crushing in November.
[Appassimento](../concepts/appassimento.md), where grapes lose water before
fermentation, can intensify the fruit and increase the potential alcohol and
residual sweetness.

That process differs from simply fermenting a normally ripe crop and leaving
some sugar. Drying changes the raw material first. Subsequent fermentation
then determines how much of its sugar becomes alcohol. The resulting balance
depends on [acidity](../concepts/acidity.md), alcohol and phenolic texture as
well as sweetness.

## More than a dessert-wine shorthand

Verduzzo's firm texture is helpful for understanding why its sweeter wines
can feel substantial rather than merely soft. Honey and dried-fruit
associations do not, by themselves, establish noble rot: drying, ripeness and
aging also produce richer aromatic profiles.

The grape remains part of [Friuli's](../regions/friuli.md) current varietal repertoire alongside
[Friulano](friulano.md) and [Ribolla Gialla](ribolla-gialla.md). A dry Verduzzo
and a Ramandolo answer different purposes, so the wine's sweetness and
production method deserve as much attention as the grape name.

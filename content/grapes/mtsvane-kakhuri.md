# Mtsvane Kakhuri

Mtsvane Kakhuri is an aromatic white Georgian wine grape associated with
Kakheti. It appears both on its own and with [Rkatsiteli](rkatsiteli.md),
adding floral and peach-like fragrance to a less aromatic partner.
Pressed white and amber versions give different expressions of that fruit.

The fuller name matters. *Mtsvane*, meaning green, occurs in several
Georgian grape names; Goruli Mtsvane is a distinct variety. “Mtsvane” on
its own can therefore require regional context, while “Kakhuri” identifies
the Kakhetian grape discussed here.

## Growing the grape

The nursery Vivai Rauscedo describes late budburst, a medium-late ripening
period, and sensitivity to summer drought. It also identifies powdery
mildew susceptibility. Winter hardiness and summer water supply are
separate considerations, so tolerance of cold should not be read as a
general ability to withstand difficult growing conditions.

## White wine and amber wine

In pressed white wines, Mtsvane Kakhuri can combine fragrance with fresh
[acidity](../concepts/acidity.md). The National Wine Agency notes its
sensitivity to [oxidation](../concepts/oxidation.md), making oxygen management important when the
intention is to preserve a fresh aromatic style. Blending with Rkatsiteli
is an established use, while Manavi is a particular regional association
for the grape.

Giuaani's Mtsvane Qvevri offers a documented example from Manavi. The winery
reports six months of fermentation and maturation with skins in clay
qvevri, followed by four months in oak barrels.[^1] This creates several
contributors to the wine's character: grape aroma, extraction from skins
and seeds, and subsequent [oak maturation](../concepts/oak-maturation.md).

Such an amber wine belongs to the wider family of
[skin-contact white wines](../styles/skin-contact-white-wine.md).
[Maceration](../concepts/maceration.md) can introduce tannic grip and deepen
colour, changing how the fruit is perceived. Comparing it with a pressed
white is consequently a comparison of methods as well as bottles.
Kakheti's continuing production of both styles gives the variety present-day
relevance within its historical home.

[^1]: Giuaani Winery, “Mtsvane Qvevri,” vineyard origin and production account.

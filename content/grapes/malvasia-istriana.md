# Malvasia Istriana

Malvasia Istriana, or Malvazija Istarska in Croatian, is a white grape strongly
associated with Istria and also grown in neighbouring northeastern Italy.
Fresh dry whites are its most familiar expression, but fuller, matured wines
are an important part of its contemporary identity.

The qualifier matters. *Malvasia* is used for numerous grapes and wines around
the Mediterranean; the word alone does not identify this particular variety.
Nor does it guarantee a sweet or strongly muscat-scented wine.

## Vineyard and structure

Rauscedo describes a very vigorous vine with thick-skinned berries and
relatively late budbreak. It favours dry, ventilated conditions and requires
attention to its abundant vegetation. Long pruning and summer work on the
canopy help distribute shoots and fruit.

The nursery's clonal accounts show meaningful variation: some selections
emphasize citrus and freshness, others more body and suitability for
maturation. [Crop load](../concepts/crop-load.md), harvest maturity and the
site all affect that balance. Malvasia Istriana can therefore offer aromatic
lift without being a uniformly exuberant, perfumed grape.

## Istria today

Istria is central to understanding the grape's present significance. The
regional tourism authority describes it as the peninsula's most widespread
white variety and places it across a varied landscape of limestone, flysch
and red soils. Those soil differences matter through water, rooting and
site conditions; colour in the soil is not a flavour recipe.

Vinistra's contemporary programme explicitly distinguishes young from aged
Malvazija. Its 2026 results include both recent-vintage wines and Kozlović's
Santa Lucia 2019 in the aged category. The useful point is the coexistence of
fresh and developed styles, rather than a ranking: this is a regional white
with several established ways of reaching the table.[^1]

In [Friuli](../regions/friuli.md), the same grape offers another route into the region's whites
alongside [Friulano](friulano.md) and [Vitovska](vitovska.md). For a bottle
with greater colour or grip, check the producer's account of
[skin contact](../styles/skin-contact-white-wine.md) and maturation. The
variety name supplies the starting point; it does not specify the cellar
method.

[^1]: Vinistra, “Champions announced as Vinistra presented in Zagreb,”
    2026 results, including separate young and aged Malvasia categories.

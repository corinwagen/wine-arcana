# Chancellor

Chancellor is a dark-skinned French-American
[hybrid grape](../concepts/hybrid-grapes.md), also known as Seibel 7053.
It belongs to the older generation of hybrids adopted in northeastern
North American vineyards. Its strong colour and productivity can be useful,
while fungal disease and crop balance require particular attention.

## A productive vine with disease constraints

Iowa State's cultivar review attributes Chancellor to Albert Seibel's
breeding at Aubenas in France. It records early budbreak, considerable
cropping potential, and susceptibility to both mildews. Cornell's older
New York guide specifically warns about early downy mildew infection of
the fruit.[^vineyard]

These traits make [crop load](../concepts/crop-load.md) and
[disease management](../concepts/disease-resistance.md) central to a planting's
performance. Maintaining healthy foliage helps the vine ripen the fruit it
carries, while early budbreak exposes young growth to
[spring frost](../concepts/spring-frost.md).

## A contemporary Finger Lakes blend

Usonia's 2025 Good Spirit makes Chancellor the largest component of a red
blend with [Baco Noir](baco-noir.md), [Traminette](traminette.md), and
[Aromella](aromella.md). Its production account distinguishes the stages:
Chancellor and the two white grapes were cofermented as whole clusters;
Baco Noir fermented separately. The lots were blended after pressing and
matured in stainless steel.

Cofermentation puts different grapes together during extraction and
fermentation, whereas
later blending combines wines whose earlier development was separate.
The two approaches can coexist in one bottling. Chancellor's role in this
wine also shows the continuing use of an older hybrid within the
[Finger Lakes](../regions/finger-lakes.md), alongside recent breeding releases.

[^vineyard]: Smiley and colleagues, Chancellor profile, revised 2016;

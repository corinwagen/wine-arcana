# Raboso Piave

Raboso Piave is a red grape of the Veneto, associated with the Piave plain
and, under the name Friularo, the Bagnoli area south of Padua. Its combination
of late ripening, substantial [acidity](../concepts/acidity.md), and
[tannin](../concepts/tannin.md) makes the grower's harvest decisions and the
cellar's handling particularly consequential.

It is distinct from [Raboso Veronese](raboso-veronese.md). Genetic work
identified the latter as an offspring of Raboso Piave and Marzemina Bianca,
rather than a regional synonym for the same vine.[^parentage]

## A long season

Vivai Cooperativi Rauscedo describes Raboso Piave as early-budding but
late-ripening, vigorous, and reliably productive. Early spring growth can
meet [frost](../concepts/spring-frost.md), while the crop needs a long
season to mature. These are separate hazards at opposite ends of
[the vineyard year](../concepts/vineyard-year.md).

The nursery notes suitability for expansive training systems, with Guyot
also possible when accompanied by careful summer canopy work. Productive
vines need enough functional leaf area to ripen their crop; a large harvest
is not automatically an advantage if it delays maturity into poor autumn
weather. Thick berry skins and susceptibility to powdery mildew add further
practical considerations.

## Managing the structure

Young wines can combine deep colour with pronounced acidity and firm tannin.
Later harvesting can make the palate rounder, but this is a choice made
within the limits of the season and fruit condition. The nursery also
describes differences between clones, including Friularo selections suited
to different styles. One harsh young wine should not be taken as the
inevitable expression of every Raboso Piave planting.

Malanotte del Piave supplies a distinctive regional response to that
structure. The specification published by Consorzio Vini Venezia requires
Raboso Piave to be the main variety and uses a portion of dried grapes,
alongside fruit vinified without drying. Its maturation requirements also
include time in wood.[^malanotte] This links
[appassimento](../concepts/appassimento.md) to the Piave plain as well as
to better-known Veronese wines.

Drying increases concentration and potential alcohol; maturation changes
tannin and aroma. Neither process simply removes acidity. Their combined
effect can make the wine feel more balanced while retaining the grape's
structural character. Malanotte is therefore one interpretation of Raboso,
not a production method implied by the grape name on every bottle.

[^malanotte]: Consorzio Vini Venezia, published Malanotte del Piave DOCG

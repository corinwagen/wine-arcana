# Rkatsiteli

Rkatsiteli is a Georgian white wine grape associated especially with
Kakheti. Its ability to retain [acidity](../concepts/acidity.md) as it
ripens helps explain its usefulness across contrasting styles: fresh
pressed whites, blends, and amber wines fermented with grape solids.

Its relatively restrained aroma also makes it a useful partner for the
more fragrant [Mtsvane Kakhuri](mtsvane-kakhuri.md). Their combination is
part of the white-wine tradition of eastern Georgia.

## Vineyard and wine

Georgia's National Wine Agency describes Rkatsiteli as late budding and
late ripening, with good winter hardiness. These traits address different
problems: winter survival and the time needed to finish ripening both
matter when selecting a site. Kakheti remains its principal Georgian
setting, with cultivation also in Kartli.

Pressed white versions can show apple, quince, or citrus flavours. When
juice ferments after separation from the skins, acidity can be the most
obvious structural feature. Fermentation with skins and seeds adds an
additional source of texture through [tannin](../concepts/tannin.md).

In the Kakhetian qvevri tradition, wine ferments in clay vessels with grape
solids and white wines may remain with them until spring. This extended
contact helps explain the amber colour and grip associated with many
Rkatsiteli wines made this way. The amount of solids and the duration of
contact matter alongside the vessel itself. The broader category of
[skin-contact white wine](../styles/skin-contact-white-wine.md) provides a
useful comparison for understanding that change in texture.

## Rkatsiteli in New York

The [Finger Lakes](../regions/finger-lakes.md) offers a contemporary example
of Rkatsiteli outside Georgia, alongside [Saperavi](saperavi.md).
Dr. Konstantin Frank grows Rkatsiteli on both its Keuka and Seneca Lake
estates and describes it as one of its last white grapes to be harvested.
That timing makes the regional example more informative than a simple
claim of cold tolerance: the vine must also ripen a crop before the
growing season ends.

For its 2025 wine, the winery reports direct pressing, cool fermentation
in stainless steel, and no [malolactic fermentation](../concepts/malolactic-fermentation.md)
or [oak ageing](../concepts/oak-maturation.md). A little
[residual sugar](../concepts/residual-sugar.md) rounds the resulting wine. This
approach offers a clear comparison with Georgian amber Rkatsiteli:
pressing and fermentation choices produce a white wine whose balance
centres on fruit, acid, and sweetness, with much less extraction from grape
solids.

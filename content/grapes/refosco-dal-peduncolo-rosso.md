# Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso

Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso is a dark-skinned grape associated especially with
[Friuli](../regions/friuli.md). The full name, referring to its red bunch stalk, is useful: Refosco
has been applied to several vines, and a bottle or planting described only
by that shorter name needs further identification.

Its wines can combine deep colour, firm [tannin](../concepts/tannin.md) and
freshness. They range from fruit-led reds to substantial wines given extended
maturation. This is one of the local grapes that makes Friuli's red-wine
landscape more varied than an account centred on Merlot would suggest.

## Ripening a generous crop

The vine buds early and ripens late in Rauscedo's technical account. That
leaves exposure to [spring frost](../concepts/spring-frost.md) at one end of
the season and a need for sufficient autumn ripening at the other. Vigorous
growth and generous cropping can further delay maturity on fertile, cool
ground.

Good exposure, appropriate pruning and management of the green canopy are
therefore consequential choices. [Crop load](../concepts/crop-load.md) must
fit the site's ability to ripen fruit; a large harvest is useful only if it
reaches the balance intended for the wine. Rauscedo also notes susceptibility
to powdery mildew and bunch-stem drying.

## Extraction and time

Dark colour does not reveal how a wine's tannins will feel. Skin contact,
ripeness and maturation all shape that texture. Ronchi di Cialla provides a
documented example of the more patient approach: its Refosco comes from the
Roncjs hillside vineyard in the Cialla valley near Prepotto, harvested by
hand in the first half of October.

The estate specifies four weeks of maceration, maturation in French and
Slavonian oak, and an extended period in bottle before release. That sequence
helps explain why this wine should be understood differently from a red
pressed early and bottled promptly for its primary fruit. The estate's long
aging predictions are producer expectations, not a promise for every Refosco.

## Regional context

Friuli Colli Orientali remains a useful point of orientation for the grape.
It sits alongside [Schioppettino](schioppettino.md), [Pignolo](pignolo.md)
and [Tazzelenghe](tazzelenghe.md) in the region's local red repertoire.
Comparing these wines from identified vineyards and producers is more
informative than treating “Friulian red” as a single style.

# Oseleta

Oseleta is a red grape of the Verona area valued for the color and structure
it can contribute to [Valpolicella](../regions/valpolicella.md) blends.
Its small, compact bunches and limited juice yield help explain why a modest
addition can make a noticeable difference to a wine based chiefly on
[Corvina](corvina.md).

## Fruit and extraction

Tenuta Santa Maria describes thick dark skins, numerous seeds, and relatively
little juice. This gives winemakers substantial solid material in relation
to the liquid they extract. [Maceration](../concepts/maceration.md) therefore
requires attention to the amount and character of the
[tannin](../concepts/tannin.md) entering the wine.

The estate also emphasizes the importance of ripeness: prematurely harvested
Oseleta can make a markedly harsh contribution. Greater color and tannin
are useful blending properties when they fit the structure of the whole wine.
They are not independent measures of quality.

## Contemporary local role

The Valpolicella consortium includes Oseleta among the area's complementary
varieties and identifies its suitability for drying. Its role consequently
extends to wines made from dried fruit, alongside wines fermented from
fresh grapes. Drying further concentrates the material entering the cellar,
so the producer's blending and extraction decisions remain central.

Oseleta is also vinified on its own, allowing its structure to become the
main subject of the wine. Tasting that expression beside a blend can clarify
what a minor variety contributes: a grape can have considerable influence
without making up most of the bottle.

# Frappato

Frappato is a dark-skinned *Vitis vinifera* grape associated with southeastern
Sicily, especially Vittoria. Its red wines often combine a relatively light
ruby colour with pronounced floral and red-fruit aromas. It is bottled on
its own and blended with [Nero d'Avola](nero-davola.md) in [Cerasuolo di
Vittoria](../regions/cerasuolo-di-vittoria.md), where the two grapes contribute
to a shared regional style.

The grape's connection with Vittoria is well documented in nineteenth-century
accounts. Its precise origin remains uncertain. Today its main Sicilian
centres include the provinces of Ragusa and Syracuse.

## Fruit, colour, and variation

Frappato can give rose or violet impressions alongside red berries and spice.
Its aromatic intensity can be striking in a wine with modest colour and
weight. [Tannin](../concepts/tannin.md), [acidity](../concepts/acidity.md), and alcohol still vary
considerably with the material planted and the way the grapes are grown
and fermented.

The Italian Vitis Database describes two biotypes with differences in
ripening, pigmentation, and aromatic compounds. Wines from one had less
alcohol and fewer phenolic compounds; those from the other showed more
colour, body, and tannic structure. Both retained appreciable acidity.
These differences help explain the range between delicate Frappato and
fuller examples without requiring a different grape name.

## Around Vittoria

Vittoria's vineyards occupy a warm Mediterranean setting between the
Hyblaean plateau and the sea. Coastal breezes and cooler nights moderate
the heat in many sites, while soil depth and underlying rock affect the
water available to vines through summer.

At Planeta's Mogli vineyard in Acate, for example, loose red sand overlies
a layer of calcareous rock. The producer identifies that underlying layer
as important to the vineyard's water supply. Its Frappato grows on a
spur-pruned trellis. Donnafugata also uses trellises for its Vittoria
Frappato, with both Guyot and spur-cordon pruning. These sites illustrate
how vineyard design and the soil beneath the surface help sustain ripening
in a dry summer.

## Varietal wines and blends

Tank maturation can leave Frappato's fruit and floral aromas prominent.
Donnafugata's Bell'Assai 2024, for example, fermented on its skins in steel
and spent about seven months in tank before bottle maturation.[^1]
The choice preserves a clear view of the grape's aroma while allowing time
for the young wine's texture to settle. Extraction and maturation can also
produce firmer wines with more emphasis on structure.

Cerasuolo di Vittoria requires 30–50% Frappato, with 50–70% Nero d'Avola.
The defined growing area covers parts of Ragusa, Caltanissetta, and Catania.[^2]
Here Frappato's fragrant character joins the colour and substance Nero
d'Avola can supply. The permitted proportions leave producers room to
adjust the blend to their vineyards and the season.

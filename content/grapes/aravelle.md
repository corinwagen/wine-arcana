# Aravelle

Aravelle is a white [hybrid grape](../concepts/hybrid-grapes.md) released
by Cornell in 2023 after decades of testing in New York. Bred from
[Cayuga White](cayuga-white.md) and [Riesling](riesling.md), it combines
Riesling-related wine possibilities with improved resistance to important
vineyard diseases. Its older trial name, NY 81, still appears in accounts
of the wines made before naming.

## Why the long trial mattered

Bruce Reisch made the cross in 1981. Cornell's release account describes
grower observations of better rot resistance than Riesling as an important
reason to continue evaluating the selection. Fruit could remain on the vine
through wet harvest conditions with less pressure for an immediate pick.
Field evaluation and winemaking trials followed that practical interest.[^origin]

The advantage concerns harvest flexibility. A healthier bunch can give the
grower more room to choose a picking date that suits sugar, acidity, and
flavour. Cornell nevertheless describes moderate resistance to mildews and
Botrytis, with continued protection required. [Disease resistance](../concepts/disease-resistance.md)
reduces susceptibility while leaving vineyard management necessary.

## Still whites and blended rosé

Cornell's 2023 account documents Weis Vineyards using NY 81 for its
semi-dry Heart of the Lake white. Usonia's 2025 Soon Different provides
a contrasting use: Aravelle joins [Regent](regent.md) and
[Aromella](aromella.md) in a rosé from Nearpass Vineyard on Keuka Lake.

Usonia pressed Regent and Aravelle together before fermentation. Aromella
received overnight skin contact and separate fermentation, and the components
were subsequently blended. A white grape can thus contribute to a pink wine's
composition without supplying its red skin pigments. The blend and processing
help determine the finished wine's balance.

Usonia also lists Aravelle among replacement vines at its New Suns vineyard
on Cayuga Lake. That planting account connects a newly named grape with
ongoing decisions about maintaining a working
[Finger Lakes](../regions/finger-lakes.md) vineyard.

[^origin]: Krishna Ramanujan, “New Cornell variety raises grape expectations
    for NYS wine,” 28 March 2023; Cornell's variety register records the full
    selection number as 81.0315.17.

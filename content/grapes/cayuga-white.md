# Cayuga White

Cayuga White is a white [hybrid grape](../concepts/hybrid-grapes.md) developed
at Cornell for New York winegrowing. It can make still wines and sparkling
wines, with harvest decisions helping determine which direction the fruit
takes. The [Finger Lakes](../regions/finger-lakes.md) supplies both its
breeding context and contemporary examples of its continuing use.

## A grape selected in New York

John Einset and W. B. Robinson's 1972 introduction records a cross made in
1945 between [Seyval Blanc](seyval-blanc.md) and Schuyler. Trials followed
at Geneva, Hammondsport and Fredonia before release. Schuyler itself
combined [Zinfandel](zinfandel.md) and Ontario ancestry, so Cayuga's family tree reaches
beyond the familiar Seyval connection.[^origin]

The original report describes vigorous, productive vines with medium
winter hardiness. Those observations help explain why a successful New
York hybrid should not automatically be treated as a candidate for the
coldest continental sites. [Winter hardiness](../concepts/winter-hardiness.md)
is relative, and severe winters can still injure buds.

## Crop, harvest and intended style

Productivity makes [crop load](../concepts/crop-load.md) an important part
of the discussion. Fruit must ripen on the vine carrying it; a high yield
in a trial does not establish a suitable target for every vineyard.
The original trials also belong to their own sites and seasons, rather
than furnishing a permanent harvest date.

Purdue's cultivar guidance describes uses ranging from fruit-led,
semi-sweet wines to dry wines and sparkling bases. Earlier picking can
retain acidity for sparkling production. That is a choice about the
starting wine: acidity that might seem assertive in one still-wine style
can provide structure after a second fermentation and maturation.

## Sparkling Cayuga today

Little Clover offers a varietal sparkling Cayuga White in its Finger Lakes
range and identifies both lees contact and disgorgement in its wine account.
It provides a contemporary example of taking this grape beyond an
everyday still white.[^clover]

The distinction is useful when reading labels. The grape supplies the
fruit and acid balance, while [lees ageing](../concepts/lees-aging.md)
introduces another source of texture and aroma. A sparkling Cayuga can
therefore differ substantially from a young still version without either
ceasing to express the variety. Compare it with
[Seyval's English sparkling role](seyval-blanc.md): related grapes can find
similar cellar uses in different regional traditions.

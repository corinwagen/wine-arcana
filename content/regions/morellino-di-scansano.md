# Morellino di Scansano

Morellino di Scansano is a red-wine appellation in the southern Tuscan
[Maremma](maremma.md), between the Ombrone and Albegna rivers. Morellino is
the local name for [Sangiovese](../grapes/sangiovese.md). The wines give that
grape a coastal-hill setting distinct from its inland Tuscan and Umbrian
homes.

The denomination covers Scansano and parts of six neighboring communes in
Grosseto province. It has its own Denominazione di Origine Controllata e
Garantita (DOCG) rules within the broader Maremma wine landscape.

## Hills and summer water

The hills descend towards the Tyrrhenian coast, with exposure and elevation
changing between sites. The official specification describes a warm, dry
climate with rain concentrated in the cooler months. Its geographical
account identifies recurrent drought as an important agricultural constraint.

Soil depth and stored water therefore matter through ripening. Morisfarms'
Poggio la Mozza estate illustrates a relatively low, sandy site near Grosseto.
Fattoria Le Pupille's 2025 Morellino combines fruit from vineyards with
textures ranging from clay-silt to sandy loam, including calcareous marl.
The appellation contains several growing conditions within its coastal
identity.

## Three release paths

The January 2026 specification requires at least 85% Sangiovese for ordinary
Morellino and Superiore, and 90% for Riserva. Superiore may be released from
1 January of the second year after harvest. Riserva requires two years of
ageing, including one in wood, counted from 1 January after harvest.[^rules]

These categories distinguish production and maturation requirements. The
producer still chooses blending grapes, extraction, and vessels within them.
A longer cellar period also changes the time available for tannin and aroma
to develop before release.

## Steel and used barrels

Fattoria Le Pupille's ordinary Morellino provides a continuing reference:
the estate records a first vintage in 1985. For its 2025 wine, Sangiovese
accounts for 90% of the blend, with Ciliegiolo and Alicante completing it.
The technical sheet describes ten to fifteen days of
[maceration](../concepts/maceration.md) and six months in steel. Keeping the
wine out of barrels focuses this example on fruit, extraction, and its
relatively early release.

Morisfarms' Riserva offers a documented contrast. Its published blend is
90% Sangiovese with Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot. Fermentation takes place
in temperature-controlled cement, with pumping over during twenty days of
skin contact, followed by a year in previously used oak barrels and subsequent
bottle maturation.

Used barrels contribute less fresh wood aroma than new ones while remaining
part of the maturation process. Comparing the two wines brings both
[oak maturation](../concepts/oak-maturation.md) and extraction into view;
the grape name alone cannot explain their different cellar paths.

[^rules]: Italian Ministry of Agriculture, specification attached to the
    decree of 28 January 2026, articles 2 and 5. The Superiore category was
    added in this revision; a March 2026 amendment also permits qualifying
    wines from the 2024 and 2025 harvests.

# Ribera del Duero

Ribera del Duero is a wine appellation along the Duero River on the northern
plateau of Spain. [Tempranillo](../grapes/tempranillo.md), locally called Tinto
Fino or Tinta del País, anchors its red wines. Rosé and whites based on Albillo
Mayor broaden a region often introduced only through oak-matured reds.

Its inland setting makes the growing season a balance between summer warmth and
a limited frost-free period. Elevation, exposure, water availability, and
harvest date matter alongside Tempranillo's local history.

## A continental growing season

The regulatory council describes a Mediterranean climate with a pronounced
continental character: dry summers, cold winters, relatively limited rainfall,
and substantial day–night temperature variation. Those conditions affect the
entire annual cycle, from dormancy and budbreak to the condition of fruit in
autumn.

[Altitude](../concepts/altitude.md) is part of the explanation, interacting with
aspect, cold-air movement, and soil. Cooler nights can help slow some changes
during ripening, but they do not cancel the effects of hot days or eliminate
spring frost. A high vineyard still needs enough warmth and time to produce the
intended fruit.

Soils and positions along the river provide further differences in rooting,
drainage, and available water.

## Tempranillo and other grapes

Tempranillo supplies the main red-wine identity. The council also lists Cabernet
Sauvignon, Merlot, Malbec, and Garnacha among the region's varieties. Permitted
proportions depend on the wine category.

Harvest fixes the starting balance of sugar, acidity, fruit condition, and
phenolics. [Maceration](../concepts/maceration.md), pressing, and maturation
then determine how much skin and seed material reaches the wine and how it
develops. A deeply coloured red can still differ markedly from its neighbour in
tannin, oak influence, or the ripeness of its fruit.

Albillo Mayor supplies a separate white-wine tradition. It should not be
confused with every Spanish grape bearing the name Albillo. The council's
current wine overview includes both younger whites and wines fermented or
matured in wood.

## Maturation

Young red wines and wines with barrel and bottle development offer different
starting points. Oak can add aroma and alter oxygen exposure, while time in
bottle continues changes in the whole wine. New and used barrels, vessel size,
and extraction influence the result alongside the duration of ageing.

Crianza, Reserva, and Gran Reserva are regulated maturation terms. The
appellation specification sets their requirements for time in barrel and bottle.

# Uco Valley

Uco Valley is an Andean wine-growing area in south-western
[Mendoza](mendoza.md), encompassing Tupungato, Tunuyán, and San Carlos. Its
wines show how [Malbec](../grapes/malbec.md), other reds, and white varieties
respond to elevation, exposure, and the soils left by rivers descending from
the mountains.

The valley's contemporary identity increasingly includes particular districts
and parcels. Gualtallary and Paraje Altamira, for example, help locate a wine
more precisely within an area whose vineyards differ in temperature, soil
depth, and available water.

## Mountains and an irrigated landscape

The Instituto Nacional de Vitivinicultura's regional report describes a dry
continental setting, with warm days, cool nights, and irrigation supplied
through the Tunuyán and Tupungato river systems. Vineyard development depends
on this water infrastructure. Rainfall over the vines is only one part of the
water supply that supports them.

[Altitude](../concepts/altitude.md) contributes to temperature differences,
but aspect and site position also change how much heat vines receive.
[Frost](../concepts/spring-frost.md), hail, and the dry Zonda wind remain
constraints. Growing higher can open possibilities for freshness while
exposing a vineyard to other seasonal risks.

Alluvial ground varies in the proportion and arrangement of sand, silt,
stones, and calcareous material. Zuccardi's technical sheet for Concreto
2022 distinguishes the Tunuyán alluvial fan at Altamira from layered sands and
calcareous material at Gualtallary. A stony profile and a deeper, finer-textured
soil can hold and release water differently, making
[irrigation](../concepts/irrigation-and-vine-water-stress.md) and rooting
important parts of any comparison.

## Grapes and the interpretation of a site

Malbec is central to Uco's red wines, with
[Cabernet Franc](../grapes/cabernet-franc.md) and
[Chardonnay](../grapes/chardonnay.md) among the other varieties that broaden
the region. Harvest date, canopy exposure, and the balance between vine growth
and crop all help determine whether ripe fruit retains sufficient
[acidity](../concepts/acidity.md).

Concreto 2022 offers one documented interpretation of Malbec from Altamira
and Gualtallary. Zuccardi used whole bunches, native-yeast fermentation, and
concrete for both fermentation and maturation, without oak. Comparing such
a wine with a barrel-matured Malbec makes cellar choices visible alongside
geography: wood contributes its own flavour and changes the conditions under
which a wine develops.

[Pinot Noir](../grapes/pinot-noir.md) provides a further contemporary
comparison. Alejandro Iglesias's August 2026 regional survey describes Uco
producers distinguishing vineyard sectors and plant material, while refining
harvest and oak use. Zorzal and Domaine Nico are among its examples. These
wines extend the valley's story beyond a single grape and show why knowing
the parcel and production method helps explain the bottle.

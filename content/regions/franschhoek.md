# Franschhoek

Franschhoek is a Cape wine-growing area where old
[Sémillon](../grapes/semillon.md) vineyards coexist with Cabernet wines and
cellars making both still and sparkling wine. Its contemporary interest lies
partly in how producers use that inherited vineyard material: vine age,
harvest date, and maturation can lead to quite different expressions.

## River ground and slopes

Boekenhoutskloof's vineyard accounts illustrate variation within the area.
Its 2023 Sémillon came from three vineyards, including flat alluvial ground
with loam and a steep, south-facing site with granite and quartz. The
vineyards were planted in 1902, 1942, and 1936 respectively.[^semillon]
Those dates document surviving plantings; they do not establish a fixed
relationship between age and wine quality.

The same producer's Franschhoek Cabernet Sauvignon draws on alluvial valley
ground and decomposed-granite slopes. Its account of 2023 describes useful
winter soil moisture followed by a moderate growing season, with later rain
requiring attention in the vineyard. Such a vintage account helps explain
why soil drainage and harvest timing matter alongside a regional climate
description.

## Sémillon and Cabernet

[Old vines](../concepts/old-vines.md) are an important part of the Sémillon
story, but cellar choices remain consequential. Boekenhoutskloof's 2023
wine used whole-bunch pressing, spontaneous fermentation, and maturation
in both barrels and concrete eggs. A small component of
[Muscat of Alexandria](../grapes/moscatel-de-alejandria.md) received skin contact.
The resulting blend brings together different fruit
and fermentation histories rather than expressing vine age alone.

For [Cabernet Sauvignon](../grapes/cabernet-sauvignon.md), the producer's
2023 approach instead involved destemming, fermentation in concrete,
pump-overs, and subsequent barrel maturation. Contact between juice and
skins builds colour and tannin; the intensity of that work helps determine
how ripe fruit becomes structure in the finished wine. Cabernet's presence
also makes neighbouring [Stellenbosch](stellenbosch.md) a useful comparison.

## A winery address and a wine's origin

Haute Cabrière provides another contemporary reference through its focus
on [Chardonnay](../grapes/chardonnay.md) and [Pinot Noir](../grapes/pinot-noir.md).
Its 2022 still Chardonnay–Pinot Noir blend
carried a Western Cape origin on the technical sheet, although the winery
is in Franschhoek. A cellar's location therefore should not be treated as
proof that every wine it makes comes entirely from local vineyards.

The regional wine association also highlights Cap Classique in its events
programme. Sparkling and still wines ask different questions of the same
grapes: picking date, juice handling, and the desired acid balance change
with the intended wine. Franschhoek is consequently useful both for studying
vineyard heritage and for understanding how a region's cellars work across
styles and grape sources.

[^semillon]: Boekenhoutskloof, *Semillon 2023*, vineyard account.

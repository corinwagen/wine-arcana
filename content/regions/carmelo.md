# Carmelo

Carmelo is a wine-growing district in western Uruguay's Colonia department,
near the meeting of the Uruguay River and the Río de la Plata. Its river
setting gives [Tannat](../grapes/tannat.md) and other red varieties a useful
regional comparison with the southern vineyards of
[Canelones](canelones.md) and the Atlantic-facing hills of
[Maldonado](maldonado.md).

## Rivers and vineyard sites

Uruguay's national wine institute, INAVI, places Colonia in a western region
with sedimentary soils of varying texture. Its account identifies river
moderation and stony sites around Carmelo and the nearby Río San Juan as
important growing conditions. These are local features within a varied
department, rather than a single soil type shared by every vineyard.

Water bodies moderate temperature, while slope, soil depth, and drainage
alter the conditions experienced by roots and fruit. A long ripening season
can suit later varieties such as
[Cabernet Sauvignon](../grapes/cabernet-sauvignon.md). The grower still has
to bring healthy fruit to harvest, balancing canopy growth, exposure, and
the weather during ripening.

## Red grapes and cellar choices

Narbona's current vineyard account places its main red-grape plantings in
Carmelo, with Tannat, [Merlot](../grapes/merlot.md),
[Cabernet Franc](../grapes/cabernet-franc.md),
[Petit Verdot](../grapes/petit-verdot.md),
[Pinot Noir](../grapes/pinot-noir.md), and
[Syrah](../grapes/syrah.md) among the varieties described. The same producer
locates white-grape vineyards farther east in Maldonado. A winery's address
therefore needs to be distinguished from the origin of each wine's fruit.

Campotinto provides a documented example of different uses of Tannat from
Carmelo's Colonia Estrella area. A December 2019 account in *Bodegas del
Uruguay* compared two 2018 wines. Tannat del Litoral used cold maceration,
fermentation, and further skin contact in steel, remaining in tank until
bottling. Gran Reserva instead included maturation in French and American
barrels of different ages.[^wines]

These methods establish different conditions for extraction and maturation
even within one producer's regional Tannat range.
[Maceration](../concepts/maceration.md) determines how much skin and seed
material enters the wine; [oak maturation](../concepts/oak-maturation.md)
then supplies another influence on texture and aroma. Tannat's substantial
structural potential can support more than one cellar approach.

## Continuity and newer production

Narbona's buildings make the relationship between history and current
production visible. The producer dates its old cellar to 1909 and its newer
winery to 2010, with vineyard planting in 1998 recorded in its account of
the estate's redevelopment.[^history] The dates describe different stages
of the property, rather than uninterrupted wine production from the same
vines throughout that period.

Carmelo consequently combines inherited wine-growing places with newer
vineyards and changing styles. Knowing the source parcel and production
method helps a reader distinguish a direct tank-made red from a more
wood-shaped wine within this river-influenced setting.

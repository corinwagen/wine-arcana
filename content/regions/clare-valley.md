# Clare Valley

Clare Valley is a South Australian wine region north of Adelaide,
especially associated with dry [Riesling](../grapes/riesling.md).
[Shiraz](../grapes/syrah.md) and
[Cabernet Sauvignon](../grapes/cabernet-sauvignon.md) are also important.
Its combination of summer warmth, cooling breezes, hills, and varied soils
supports both brisk white wines and substantial reds.

## Warmth and local cooling

Wine Australia identifies warm-to-hot summer conditions moderated by
afternoon breezes, with altitude and aspect creating differences among
vineyards. Limestone below Watervale and broken slate around Polish Hill
River provide two useful soil references. Rooting depth and water storage
help translate those profiles into conditions experienced by the vines.

Riesling's prominence shows why a warm regional climate can still produce
an acid-led white. The fruit's exposure, the weather during ripening,
and the picking date work together. A grower selects the harvest balance
before fermentation determines whether the finished wine retains sugar.

## Comparing Riesling vineyards

Grosset provides a contemporary comparison through Polish Hill and
Springvale. Its 2026 Polish Hill account identifies a vineyard established
in 1996 on hard slate, with hand harvesting and free-run juice. The
Springvale 2024 account describes red loam over limestone and slate.
Reading the site descriptions together establishes different vineyard
sources without assigning one fixed flavour to each rock type.

[Pressing](../concepts/pressing.md) is a further choice. Selecting free-run
juice limits the role of harder pressing, which can recover juice with a
different balance of skin-derived material. The resulting wine reflects
both the harvested fruit and the juice fraction retained.

Jim Barry's Florita vineyard gives another view of continuity and change.
The producer traces its Watervale site from Palomino planting in 1946 to
Riesling in 1962, and describes the role of its grapes in John Vickery's
Leo Buring wines.[^florita] A surviving vineyard can therefore carry a
history of changing grape and wine objectives.

## Bottle development and regional breadth

Jim Barry's 2013 Florita cellar release illustrates a Riesling offered with
several years of [bottle age](../concepts/bottle-aging.md). Mature aroma
develops through reactions in the bottled wine. Harvest balance, storage,
and the [closure](../concepts/wine-closures.md) affect the course of that
development, alongside the drinker's preference for youthful or mature fruit.

Clare's Shiraz and Cabernet add a separate structural comparison. Skin
fermentation and maturation create reds with tannin and weight, while the
Riesling examples show how growers and cellars preserve and develop white
fruit. [Eden Valley](eden-valley.md) provides another South Australian
setting in which the same principal grapes meet different terrain and
ripening conditions.

[^florita]: Jim Barry Wines, “2013 The Florita Riesling,” vineyard history.

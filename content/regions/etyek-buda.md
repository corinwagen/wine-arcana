# Etyek-Buda

Etyek-Buda is a Hungarian wine district west and southwest of Budapest,
associated with fresh white wines and grapes for sparkling wine. Its history
connects the vineyards around Etyek with the cellars and wine trade of
Budafok, now part of Budapest. The district also includes the Buda and
Velence areas, so its name covers several landscapes.

## Cool sites and different foundations

Limestone and dolomite occur around Etyek and Buda, with varying coverings of
loess and clay; the Velence hills also bring granite into the picture. The
regional agency emphasizes the district's relatively cool conditions and air
movement. These help explain the prominence of whites that retain noticeable
[acidity](../concepts/acidity.md).

[Chardonnay](../grapes/chardonnay.md),
[Sauvignon Blanc](../grapes/sauvignon-blanc.md),
[Riesling](../grapes/riesling.md), and
[Pinot Gris](../grapes/pinot-gris.md) provide different expressions of that
freshness. [Pinot Noir](../grapes/pinot-noir.md) contributes both still red
wine and a connection with sparkling production.

## From base wine to estate bottlings

József Törley's establishment of a sparkling-wine business at Budafok in 1882
created an important market for Etyek grapes. That history remains useful for
understanding why the district values acidity and why a vineyard's location
need not match the location of the cellar that processes its crop.

Etyeki Kúria offers a contemporary example of the district's broader range:
its Etyek wines include varietal Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, and Pinot Noir,
alongside a sparkling-wine operation. The producer also works in
[Sopron](sopron.md); readers should distinguish the origins of its individual
bottlings. Across the district, still and sparkling wines turn a similar
climatic starting point into different textures and styles.

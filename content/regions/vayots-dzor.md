# Vayots Dzor

Vayots Dzor is a mountainous wine region in southeastern Armenia, centered
on valleys including that of the Arpa River. It is a central contemporary
home of [Areni](../grapes/areni.md) red wine and
[Voskehat](../grapes/voskehat.md) white wine. Its significance includes both
old vineyard material and producers developing distinct modern expressions
of local grapes.

## Mountain vineyards

The region contains large differences in height, exposure, and terrain.
Vineyards occupy valleys and slopes rather than the entire mountainous
landscape. Dry conditions and elevation shape the growing season, but
ripening, autumn rain, and winter cold remain practical concerns.
[Altitude](../concepts/altitude.md) is one part of that environment, alongside
sun exposure and the site's ability to supply water.

A specific vineyard example is more informative than a single regional
number. Storica's technical sheet for Zulal's 2019 Voskehat identifies fruit
from Aghavnadzor at about 1,400 metres, on a south-facing plot with limestone
and volcanic material. It records old, own-rooted vines. Those details
describe the source of that wine; they should not be generalized to every
vineyard in Vayots Dzor.

## Local grapes in contemporary wine

Zulal's Voskehat was fermented in stainless steel and bottled from tank,
giving readers an example in which the grape's fruit and texture can be
considered without barrel-derived flavor. Areni supplies a red-wine
counterpart, with producers making different choices about extraction and
maturation.

The region's contemporary importance comes from those continuing choices:
which local grapes to cultivate, which parcels to preserve, and what wines
to make from them. Its mountain setting connects these wines, while the
grape and producer determine much of their individual character.

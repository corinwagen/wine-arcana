# Maipo Valley

The Maipo Valley is a central Chilean wine region around Santiago, extending
from the Andean foothills toward the coastal range. Its best-known red grape is
[Cabernet Sauvignon](../grapes/cabernet-sauvignon.md). Understanding a Maipo
Cabernet starts with its vineyard: the broad valley contains different soils,
exposures, and water supplies, and the foothill vineyards represent one part of
that landscape.

## Foothills, soils, and water

Puente Alto provides a documented example of the eastern foothill setting.
Don Melchor's vineyard lies on the northern bank of the Maipo River, where a
shallow loamy surface overlies stones and sandy loam. The producer's 2019
technical sheet places it at about 650 metres.[^site] Such a profile makes
rooting depth and access to stored water important. A stony surface alone tells
a grower less than the sequence of layers beneath it.

The contrast between a dry summer and winter rain makes water management a
practical part of the region's wine identity. Don Melchor's account of its
current farming describes precision drip irrigation alongside efforts to
improve soil water retention. Those practices address different parts of the
problem: irrigation supplies water, while the soil determines how much remains
accessible to roots. The wider relationship is explained in
[irrigation and vine water stress](../concepts/irrigation-and-vine-water-stress.md).

A regional climate description also needs a vintage. At Puente Alto, the 2019
growing season brought limited winter rain, warm summer conditions, and a cooler
period late in ripening. A vineyard that can ripen Cabernet reliably still
requires harvest decisions responsive to that year's heat and water supply.

## Grapes and cellar choices

Cabernet's importance gives Maipo a clear identity, but the wines include
blends and white varieties. Don Melchor's 2019 wine combined Cabernet Sauvignon
with smaller amounts of [Cabernet Franc](../grapes/cabernet-franc.md),
[Merlot](../grapes/merlot.md), and
[Petit Verdot](../grapes/petit-verdot.md). Its documented method used tank
fermentation, further skin contact, and French-barrel maturation. Extraction
establishes part of the wine's [tannin](../concepts/tannin.md) structure; wood
then changes its texture and aromatic frame.

De Martino's range supplies another point of entry. Its Maipo wines include
Cabernet and [Carménère](../grapes/carmenere.md), as well as
[Sauvignon Blanc](../grapes/sauvignon-blanc.md) and
[Sémillon](../grapes/semillon.md). This breadth matters when reading a regional
label: Maipo identifies the growing area, while the grape and cellar method
explain whether the bottle is a white, a varietal red, or a structured blend.

For comparison, [Colchagua](colchagua-valley.md) offers another central Chilean
red-wine landscape, while [Casablanca](casablanca-valley.md) makes Pacific
cooling more prominent in its white wines and Pinot Noir.
